# Room Type Library: Functional & Aesthetic Guidance for Multimodal Models

This library provides a comprehensive understanding of various room types, including their typical functions, common furniture layouts, and aesthetic characteristics. It guides multimodal models in interpreting room labels from floor plans and enriching 3D renders with appropriate furnishing and styling.

## I. Residential Room Types

-   **Living Room:**
    -   **Function:** Relaxation, entertainment, social gathering.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Sofa, armchairs, coffee table, side tables, entertainment unit, rugs, lamps.
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `cozy`, `spacious`, `inviting`, `modern`, `classic`, `open-plan`
    -   **Suggested Styling:** `decorative cushions`, `bookshelves`, `artwork`

-   **Bedroom:**
    -   **Function:** Rest, sleep, personal space.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Bed (king, queen, full, twin), nightstands, dresser, wardrobe, armchair (optional), desk (optional).
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `serene`, `private`, `comfortable`, `minimalist`, `luxurious`
    -   **Suggested Styling:** `soft bedding`, `curtains`, `reading lamp`

-   **Kitchen:**
    -   **Function:** Cooking, food preparation, dining (if eat-in).
    -   **Common Furniture:** Cabinets (upper & lower), countertop, sink, stove/oven, refrigerator, dining table/island with chairs/stools.
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `functional`, `modern`, `sleek`, `rustic`, `spacious`
    -   **Suggested Styling:** `kitchen appliances`, `hanging lights`, `potted herbs`

-   **Dining Room:**
    -   **Function:** Formal or informal dining.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Dining table, dining chairs, sideboard/buffet.
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `elegant`, `formal`, `intimate`, `bright`
    -   **Suggested Styling:** `centerpiece`, `wall art`, `chandelier`

-   **Bathroom:**
    -   **Function:** Personal hygiene.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Vanity with sink, toilet, shower/bathtub, mirror, storage.
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `clean`, `minimalist`, `spa-like`, `functional`
    -   **Suggested Styling:** `towels`, `small plants`, `bath products`

-   **Home Office / Study:**
    -   **Function:** Work, study, reading.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Desk, office chair, bookshelf, filing cabinet.
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `productive`, `calm`, `organized`, `contemporary`
    -   **Suggested Styling:** `task lamp`, `computer monitor`, `potted plant`

## II. Other Room Types

-   **Hallway / Corridor:**
    -   **Function:** Passage between rooms.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Console table, mirror, runner rug (optional).
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `transitional`, `welcoming`, `functional`
-   **Entryway / Foyer:**
    -   **Function:** Transition space, welcoming guests.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Console table, mirror, coat rack, bench (optional).
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `inviting`, `organized`, `first impression`
-   **Laundry Room:**
    -   **Function:** Washing and drying clothes.
    -   **Common Furniture:** Washer, dryer, utility sink, folding counter, storage cabinets.
    -   **Aesthetic Keywords:** `functional`, `efficient`, `clean`

---

**General Principles for Multimodal Models:**

-   **Contextual Furniture Placement:** Furniture should be logically placed within rooms, allowing for circulation and functionality (e.g., bed against a wall, sofa facing entertainment).
-   **Style Consistency:** Ensure all furniture and styling elements match the overall `user_style_preferences.md`.
-   **Dimension Awareness:** When dimensions are available, ensure furniture placement respects room size and scale.
