# Furniture Style Catalog: Visual Reference for 3D Renders

This catalog provides a visual and descriptive library of various furniture styles, pieces, and aesthetic characteristics. It guides the image generation model in accurately rendering furniture that matches a user's specified style preferences and functional requirements within a 3D floor plan.

## I. Modern Style Furniture

-   **Characteristics:** Clean lines, minimalist aesthetic, often uses materials like metal, glass, and neutral fabrics. Focus on functionality and simplicity.
-   **Keywords:** `sleek modern sofa`, `minimalist coffee table`, `glass dining table`, `geometric shapes`, `neutral color palette`
-   **Example Pieces:**
    -   **Sofa:** Low-profile, modular, neutral upholstery.
    -   **Chair:** Eames Lounge Chair (stylized representation), simple dining chairs.
    -   **Table:** Round or rectangular glass/metal coffee table.

## II. Mid-Century Modern Style Furniture

-   **Characteristics:** Organic shapes, clean lines, integration of natural and man-made materials, often warm wood tones, vibrant accent colors.
-   **Keywords:** `mid-century modern armchair`, `teak credenza`, `sputnik chandelier`, `tapered legs`, `organic curves`
-   **Example Pieces:**
    -   **Sofa:** Tufted, with exposed wooden frame.
    -   **Chair:** Eames Molded Plywood Chair, Wishbone Chair.
    -   **Table:** Noguchi coffee table.

## III. Classic / Traditional Style Furniture

-   **Characteristics:** Rich wood tones, ornate details, classic silhouettes, often upholstered in luxurious fabrics, symmetrical arrangements.
-   **Keywords:** `ornate traditional sofa`, `cherry wood dining set`, `claw-foot table`, `tufted upholstery`, `dark wood finish`
-   **Example Pieces:**
    -   **Sofa:** Chesterfield sofa, Queen Anne style.
    -   **Chair:** Wingback chair, Chippendale dining chair.
    -   **Table:** Pedestal dining table.

## IV. Industrial Style Furniture

-   **Characteristics:** Raw, unfinished materials (exposed brick, metal, reclaimed wood), utilitarian design, robust and functional.
-   **Keywords:** `metal framed desk`, `reclaimed wood dining table`, `exposed pipe shelving`, `distressed finishes`, `factory style`
-   **Example Pieces:**
    -   **Table:** Metal base with thick wood top.
    -   **Chair:** Tolix-style chair.
    -   **Shelving:** Pipe and wood shelving unit.

## V. Minimalist Style Furniture

-   **Characteristics:** Utmost simplicity, clean lines, monochrome or limited color palettes, uncluttered spaces, emphasis on form and negative space.
-   **Keywords:** `ultra-minimalist bed frame`, `simple geometric shapes`, `sparse furnishing`, `white grey black palette`, `uncluttered space`
-   **Example Pieces:**
    -   **Sofa:** Simple box-shaped, no embellishments.
    -   **Bed:** Platform bed.
    -   **Storage:** Integrated, handle-less cabinets.

## VI. Boho / Bohemian Style Furniture

-   **Characteristics:** Eclectic mix of textures, global influences, natural materials (rattan, macrame), comfortable and relaxed feel, often colorful.
-   **Keywords:** `rattan armchair`, `macrame wall hanging`, `patterned rug`, `eclectic decor`, `natural fibers`
-   **Example Pieces:**
    -   **Sofa:** Low-slung, with many cushions.
    -   **Chair:** Papasan chair, wicker.
    -   **Decor:** Moroccan poufs, woven baskets.

---

**General Principles for Furniture Rendering:**

-   **Scale & Proportion:** Ensure furniture is rendered to scale within the room, maintaining realistic proportions.
-   **Functional Placement:** Furniture should be placed logically, allowing for movement and use within the room.
-   **Material Accuracy:** Textures (wood grain, fabric weave, metal sheen) should accurately reflect the chosen style.
-   **Lighting Integration:** Furniture should respond to the scene's lighting, casting appropriate shadows and highlights.
-   **Consistency:** All furniture within a single render should adhere to the specified style.
