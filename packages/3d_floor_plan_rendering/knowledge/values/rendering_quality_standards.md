# Rendering Quality Standards: Criteria for 3D Floor Plan Sketches

This document defines the quality standards and criteria for evaluating 3D furnished floor plan sketches generated by the image generation model. Adherence to these standards ensures consistent, high-quality, and usable visual output for architectural visualization.

## I. Stylistic Adherence

-   **Style Consistency:** The rendered image must flawlessly adhere to the specified rendering style (e.g., `sketchy architectural drawing`, `photorealistic`, `minimalist line art`). Any deviation in line quality, color application, or shading model is a failure.
-   **Camera Angle Accuracy:** The camera angle (e.g., `3/4 isometric`, `top-down`) must accurately reflect the request, providing the intended spatial understanding.

## II. Architectural Accuracy

-   **Plan Interpretation Fidelity:** The 3D render must accurately represent the layout, room boundaries, and architectural elements (walls, doors, windows) identified from the original 2D floor plan.
-   **Scale & Proportion:** All architectural elements and furniture must be rendered at a consistent and realistic scale relative to each other and the overall space. Objects should not appear distorted or disproportionate.

## III. Furniture & Furnishing Quality

-   **Functional Placement:** Furniture must be placed logically and functionally within the rooms, allowing for clear circulation paths and ease of use, adhering to `architectural_design_principles.md`.
-   **Stylistic Cohesion:** All furnished items must strictly adhere to the requested furniture style (e.g., `modern`, `mid-century`, `classic`), as defined in `furniture_style_catalog.md`.
-   **Material Representation:** Materials for furniture, floors, and walls should be rendered accurately according to the chosen style (e.g., wood grain visible if requested, polished metal sheen).

## IV. Visual Clarity & Composition

-   **Readability:** The layout, room types, and key furniture items must be clearly distinguishable and easily understood.
-   **Compositional Balance:** The overall composition should be aesthetically pleasing and balanced, without awkward cropping or cluttered areas.
-   **Lighting & Shadow:** Lighting should be consistent with the specified scheme and contribute to clarity, defining form without obscuring details. Shadows should be naturalistic for the chosen style.

## V. Technical Quality

-   **Resolution & Sharpness:** The output image must meet the specified resolution and be sharp and clear throughout, without blurriness or pixelation.
-   **Absence of Artifacts:** The image should be free from generation artifacts, noise, or unintended distortions.
-   **Cleanliness:** No unwanted elements, smudges, or visual distractions.

## VI. Negative Constraints Adherence

-   **Absence of Forbidden Traits:** The render must exhibit no signs of traits that were explicitly negated in the prompt (e.g., `no floating objects`, `no unrealistic shadows`, `no clutter`).

---

**General Quality Assurance Principles:**

-   **Automated Verification:** Ideally, key quality checks can be automated by a multimodal model comparing the render to the original prompt and structured plan data.
-   **User Feedback Integration:** Incorporate user feedback for iterative improvements to rendering quality and prompt refinement strategies.
-   **Consistency Across SKUs:** For generating multiple renders, consistency in quality and style adherence is paramount.
